BIRIYANI

home made biriyani

ingredients

  • chicken
  • rice
  • all spices
  • lemon
  • salt
  • turmaric
  • ghee

Instructions

  1. first marinate the chicken
  2. next pour the oil and ghee into the bowl
  3. fry the sliced onions until golden brown colour
  4. add the marinated chicken into yhe bowl and cook on the medium heat for 8-10 mins
  5. add salt and whole spices
  6. add the drained soaked rice on high heat for 5-6 mins
  7. until 85-90% cooked the rice should still have slight bite to it
  8. drain the water immediately
  9. layer the cooked rice evenly over the chicken
